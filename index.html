Why a specialist agency

We only work with med spas. That's the entire point.

Med spa marketing has its own rhythm — consult cycles, seasonal treatment demand, high no-show rates, and patients who research for weeks before booking. A generalist agency learns this on your budget. We already know it.

That focus shows up in the details: ad creative built around injectables, body contouring and skin treatments specifically, booking flows that account for consultation-before-purchase behavior, and follow-up sequences timed to how med spa leads actually decide.
