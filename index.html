Why a specialist

We work with med spas, and only med spas.

Med spa clients research for weeks, ask for a consult before a treatment, and often send their inquiry in the evening or at the weekend. Too many of those inquiries wait until morning, and too many booked consults never show up.

Everything we build is shaped around those moments: ads for injectables, skin and body treatments, fast replies to new inquiries, and reminders that bring people through the door.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who do you work with?

Only medical spas. We take on no other industries, so everything we build is shaped around how med spa clients research and book.

What happens on the walkthrough?

It’s a free 30-minute conversation about how you get new clients today and where inquiries are lost. You leave knowing whether a system would help, with no obligation.

Do you promise specific results?

No. We don’t promise numbers or bookings. What we do is make sure every inquiry gets a fast reply and every booked consult gets a reminder.

How do you handle patient information?

We keep it minimal: name, phone, email and appointment time only, never treatment or health details. If your clinic needs a Business Associate Agreement, we’ll discuss it before any patient information is entered.

How does the deposit work?

It’s optional. Some clinics collect a small refundable deposit at booking to filter out casual inquiries. We can set it up if you want it, and leave it out if you don’t.

What does it cost?

It depends on the clinic and the length of commitment, so we agree it with you on the call.
