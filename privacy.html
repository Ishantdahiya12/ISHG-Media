Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information ISHG Media (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Contact: ishant@ishgmedia.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, clinic name and anything you write when you book a call, email us or reply to us.
  • Booking details, such as the time you choose, which are collected through our scheduling provider (Setmore).
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit this site. We don’t use advertising cookies on this site at the moment. If that changes, we will update this page.

How we use it

To reply to you, schedule and hold calls, send information you asked for, run our business, and keep our records. We contact business owners about our services by email. Every message gives you a way to say no, and we stop when you ask.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We use service providers to run our business, such as Setmore (scheduling), Microsoft 365 (email) and GoHighLevel (client systems). They handle information only to provide their service to us. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Information we handle for clinics

When we work with a med spa, we handle its inquiry and appointment contacts on its behalf. We ask clinics to enter only a person’s name, phone number, email and appointment date and time, and never treatment, medical or health details. The clinic is responsible for having consent to contact its patients. We protect the account with two-step login and restricted access, and we delete a client’s contact data within 7 days after our work ends. If a clinic needs a Business Associate Agreement, we discuss it before any patient information is entered.

How long we keep it

We keep general business records for as long as we need them to run our business and meet legal duties, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop emailing you, by writing to ishant@ishgmedia.com.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
